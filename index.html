About

How I work

I did my Master of Physiotherapy in Neurosciences at Dr. D. Y. Patil College, Pune. The course covers motor control, motor learning, balance and coordination, and what goes wrong with each of them after a stroke or a spinal injury, or in something like Parkinson's.

Neurological recovery is slow and it rarely runs in a straight line. Most of it depends on what the patient does between sessions, which is why I treat people where they live. Bed height, stair depth, the layout of a bathroom, the width of a doorway: those are what decide whether something can actually be practised. A plan built around them lasts better than one written for a treatment room.

I'm taking new patients at the moment, including complex cases. If you're not sure physiotherapy will help, message me before you book. I would rather say so than start something that won't do much.

Working with your medical team

  • Standard outcome measures at assessment and again at review, so progress is measured, not guessed.
  • Written progress notes for the treating doctor on request.
  • After surgery I work according to the operating surgeon's protocol.
  • Any new or worsening neurological sign and I stop, and refer back that day.

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. One free assessment per patient, in the areas listed above. You're not obliged to book anything after it, and if physiotherapy isn't the right answer for the condition I'll say so.

What do sessions cost, and how do I pay?

₹800 to ₹1,000 a session, depending on the condition and your area. Longer packages work out cheaper. Travel is included in the areas listed above.

How long is a session, and how many will I need?

Sessions run 45 to 60 minutes and the first visit takes a bit longer. How many you'll need depends on the condition and how long ago it started. You'll get an estimate and a set of goals in writing after the assessment.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No, you can contact me directly. If a doctor is already treating the patient I'm happy to keep them in the loop.

What should I have ready for the first visit?

Recent reports, scans or the discharge summary if you have them. Loose clothing. A clear space ideally near a bed or a sturdy chair.

Do you bring equipment?

Yes, I bring what the session needs. If you'll need something longer term, like a walker or resistance bands, I'll tell you what to buy and from where.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Let me know as early as you can and we'll find another slot.

What are your timings?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sundays by arrangement.
